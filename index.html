1. Overview

WMS (Winterman Music Spectrum) is an Android music player, spectrum visualization, synchronization, and media export application.

WMS can play user-selected music, generate animated spectrum visualizations, display user-selected image or video backgrounds, synchronize lyrics, quotes and text, save local project data, and generate media exports.

Core music processing, visualization, synchronization, project processing, and media export operations are designed to take place primarily on the user's device.

WMS does not currently provide a WMS cloud account or WMS-operated cloud storage for uploading personal music, backgrounds, lyrics, projects, or exported media.

2. Information and media WMS may access

The information WMS accesses depends on the features you choose to use.

Audio and music files

Purpose
Music playback, spectrum visualization, lyric synchronization, quote synchronization, project creation, and export.
Processing
Primarily processed locally on your device.
WMS cloud
WMS does not upload your selected music to a WMS-operated cloud server.

Images and videos

Purpose
Background media for preview, visualization, projects, and exports.
Processing
User-selected images and videos are processed locally for WMS features.
Sharing
They are not shared by WMS unless you explicitly choose to share an exported file through another application or service.

Audio visualization data

Purpose
Generate real-time spectrum visualization from audio playback.
Recording
WMS does not intentionally create or store microphone voice recordings as part of the spectrum feature.
Upload
Spectrum analysis data is not uploaded to a WMS-operated server.

Lyrics, quotes and text

Purpose
Tap-to-Sync, quote templates, timing, captions, project editing, and exported overlays.
Storage
Stored locally when needed for WMS projects or generated lyric data.

Project information

Examples
Project settings, spectrum mode, background selection, timing, quote plans, export range, orientation, and visual settings.
Storage
Stored locally on your device to support project save and resume.

Exported media

Purpose
Save content created by you using WMS.
Storage
Exported files are saved to device storage or another location selected by you.

5. Android permissions

WMS may use Android permissions or system capabilities depending on Android version and the feature being used.

Audio / Media access Used to allow you to select and play music stored on your device.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO or legacy storage access Used on applicable Android versions to access audio files selected for playback and WMS projects.
RECORD_AUDIO Android may require this permission for certain audio visualization or playback analysis functions. WMS uses this access for spectrum visualization and does not intentionally save microphone voice recordings as part of this feature.
Foreground media playback Used to allow music playback and media controls to continue appropriately while WMS is not in the foreground.
Bluetooth access Where supported and required by Android, this may be used for interaction with Bluetooth audio devices and media playback.
Phone state Where used, phone-state information may support playback interruption or audio behavior during calls. WMS does not use this permission to read call content.

You can review or revoke supported permissions at any time through Android system settings.

Revoking a permission may cause the related WMS feature to stop working.

6. How WMS uses information

Information accessed by WMS is used to provide, maintain, and improve functions requested by the user, including:

  • Music playback.
  • Spectrum visualization.
  • Lyrics and quote synchronization.
  • Project save and resume.
  • Background media processing.
  • Media export.
  • Purchase and subscription access.
  • Advertising in the Free version.
  • Security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and performance where provided by integrated Google services.

7. Data sharing

WMS does not sell your personal music files, selected backgrounds, lyrics, quote text, project files, or locally generated exports.

Certain information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide their functions.

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

Used for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention in versions of WMS that display ads.

Google Play Billing

Used for purchases, subscriptions, purchase verification, entitlement management, and related transaction functions.

Android and Google Play services

May provide operating system, media, security, store, licensing, and platform functions required by the app.

If you explicitly share exported media through another application, messaging service, cloud storage provider, social network, website, or other service, that service's privacy practices apply to the information you choose to share.

8. Local storage and data retention

WMS may store app settings, project information, cached data, generated lyric timing, quote timing, and other feature-related information locally on your device.

Local app data generally remains on the device until one of the following occurs:

  • You delete the relevant project or content.
  • You clear WMS app data.
  • You uninstall WMS.
  • Android removes temporary application data.

Exported media remains in the storage location selected by you until you delete or move it.

Information processed by Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, or other Google services is subject to the retention practices of those services.

9. Data deletion

WMS does not currently require a WMS cloud account.

Therefore, there is currently no WMS-hosted user profile containing your personal music or project library that must be deleted from a WMS server.

To remove locally stored WMS information, you may:

  • Delete projects or exported files.
  • Clear WMS storage through Android settings.
  • Uninstall WMS.

Data associated with Google Play purchases or Google advertising services is managed according to Google's applicable account, payment, advertising, privacy, and retention policies.

10. Security

WMS is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of user-created media by performing core music, spectrum, synchronization, project, and export operations primarily on the device.

Third-party services integrated into WMS may transmit information using their own secure communication methods.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No digital system, network transmission, or storage mechanism can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's privacy

WMS is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

The application's Google Play target audience, advertising configuration, and consent implementation must remain consistent with applicable Google Play requirements and applicable laws.

If WMS is later offered specifically to children or configured for child-directed use, the app configuration and this Privacy Policy will be updated as required.
