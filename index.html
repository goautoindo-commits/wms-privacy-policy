WMS · Winterman Music Spectrum

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WMS (Winterman Music Spectrum) accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Android application.

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Last updated: September 29, 2026

Public Google Play Privacy Policy

1. Overview

WMS is a music player and music-visualization application.

WMS can play audio, generate spectrum visualizations, use user-selected images or videos as backgrounds, synchronize text and quotes, save local projects, and export media.

Music files, selected media, visualization processing, project information, and generated exports are primarily processed locally on your device.

WMS does not operate a cloud service for uploading your personal music or project files.

2. Information WMS accesses

Information / Access Purpose Handling
Audio or music files selected by you Playback, visualization, synchronization, and export Processed locally on the device. WMS does not upload selected music to a WMS server.
User-selected images or videos Backgrounds for preview and exported content Processed locally on the device unless you choose to share exported media through another service.
Audio analysis permission Analyze playback audio for spectrum visualization WMS does not intentionally store voice recordings and does not upload analyzed audio to a WMS server.
Local projects, quote timing, settings, and preferences Save and resume projects and restore configuration Stored locally in app storage on your device.
Files generated by WMS MP4 or media export Saved to your device at your request.
Purchase and entitlement information Activate, restore, or verify paid access Processed through Google Play Billing.
Advertising and device data in the Free version Advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention May be processed by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The Free version of WMS may display advertising using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, SDK configuration, and Google's services, the advertising SDK may collect or process information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, including advertising or app-scoped identifiers where available;
  • app and advertisement interactions such as app launches, taps, impressions, or video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information such as crashes, app performance, SDK performance, hang rate, or energy usage.

Google may process this information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
