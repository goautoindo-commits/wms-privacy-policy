1. Overview

WMS is a music player, spectrum visualization, synchronization, and media export application. It can play music, show image or video backgrounds, synchronize lyrics and quotes, save a local project, and export videos.

Music playback, spectrum analysis, editing, and media export take place on your device. WMS does not provide a WMS cloud account or a WMS-operated server for uploading your music, backgrounds, lyrics, quotes, projects, or exports.

Advertising involves Google services, and Android may back up eligible app data. These activities are described in section 3 and section 8.

2. Information and media WMS accesses

The information WMS accesses depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

Audio and music library

Access
With audio-library permission, WMS reads the device's audio catalogue, including file names, durations, and file references, to show your music library. You can also select an individual audio file through Android's file picker.
Purpose
Playback, playlists, spectrum visualization, synchronization, project resume, and export.
Processing
Audio and library information are processed locally. WMS does not send your music files or music library to an advertising service or a WMS server.

Images and videos

Access
You choose a background image or video through Android's file picker. WMS may retain read access to the selected file so it can reopen the background or resume a project.
Purpose
Local previews, backgrounds, visualization, and exported videos. WMS does not request broad photo or video library permissions for this selection.

Spectrum visualization

Access
WMS uses Android's Visualizer to analyse audio from its own music player's audio session.
Microphone
Android requires the permission named RECORD_AUDIO for this API. WMS does not use it to record your microphone or conversations. The spectrum feature does not upload analysis data.

Lyrics, quotes, and project information

Examples
Text you enter or import, lyric timing, quote timing and styles, selected audio title and file reference, background reference, spectrum settings, orientation, and export range.
Storage
Settings and the saved project are stored locally. Generated lyric and text files can be saved in shared device storage. Saved file references help WMS reopen media; they are not a WMS cloud copy of the media.

Exported media

Storage
Exported MP4 videos are saved in your device's Movies/Winterman Music Spectrum folder. Generated lyric and text files use WMS folders in Documents.
Sharing
WMS does not automatically publish these files. If you share them using another app or service, that service handles the content you choose to share.

If you select a file from a cloud provider shown in Android's file picker, that provider may retrieve the file using its own service. Its privacy practices apply to that retrieval.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

WMS uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display a banner. The current version uses Google's test advertising configuration.

Google discloses that its SDK automatically collects and shares these categories for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address: may indicate your general location.
  • Interactions: app launches and interactions with ads.
  • Diagnostics: information about app and SDK performance.
  • Identifiers: advertising ID, app set ID, and applicable account-related identifiers.

Availability depends on your device and advertising settings. WMS does not request GPS location permission for its banner, or supply your selected music, backgrounds, lyrics, or quote text to ad requests.

File selection and permission choices

Images, videos, audio, and lyric documents can be opened through Android's file picker. Access is granted to the selected document, and WMS may retain that read grant to reopen it later.

The current WMS app does not request camera, GPS location, contacts, SMS, call-log, phone-state, or Bluetooth discovery/connection permissions. Background selection does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, or all-files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

You can review supported permissions under Android Settings → Apps → WMS → Permissions. Names vary by device. Denying audio-library access limits library browsing; denying spectrum access affects visualization. Revoking access to a file may require selecting it again.

6. How information is used

WMS uses local media, text, and settings to provide the features you select: music playback, visualization, lyric and quote timing, project save/resume, background previews, and media export.

Google handles advertising-related information as described in section 3. WMS does not operate its own cloud analytics or media-upload service.

7. Data sharing

WMS does not sell your personal music files, selected backgrounds, lyrics, quotes, project files, or exports. It does not automatically upload this content to a WMS server.

  • Google advertising services: receive SDK data described in section 3.
  • Android and your backup provider: may handle eligible app data during backup, restore, or device transfer, as described in section 8.
  • Services you choose: a file provider or another app handles files that you select, open, or share through that service.

Publicly saved files may also be accessible to other apps with appropriate storage access. The privacy practices of each service apply to information it receives.

8. Local storage, backup, and retention

WMS keeps settings and its saved project in app storage. The project can include selected media references, the audio title, quote text, timing, and visual settings. Temporary processing files may be stored in the app's cache.

Saved app data remains until you replace or delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall WMS. Temporary files may be removed by WMS or Android. Original media, generated lyrics/text, and exported videos in shared storage remain until you delete them separately.

Android backup is enabled

Depending on your device and backup settings, Android can back up eligible WMS settings and app-private project data to your backup provider, or transfer them to another device. They may be restored when you reinstall WMS or set up a device. This is handled by Android and your provider; WMS does not operate a backup server.

You can manage available backup and restore options in your device settings. Deleting local app data may not remove copies held by a backup provider.

Information handled by Google advertising services is retained under Google's policies. Information you submit through the public support repository remains subject to GitHub's controls and policies.

9. How to delete your data

WMS does not provide user accounts or a WMS-hosted media library, so there is no WMS cloud account to delete.

  1. Saved project: use the project's delete action in WMS to remove the saved project. Original media and exported files are separate.
  2. App settings and private data: open Android Settings → Apps → WMS → Storage, then choose Clear storage/Clear data, or uninstall WMS. Clearing cache alone does not remove all saved app data.
  3. Shared files: use your file manager or gallery to delete WMS-generated lyric/text files in Documents and exported videos in Movies/Winterman Music Spectrum. Uninstalling WMS does not necessarily remove them.
  4. Backups and shared copies: manage or delete them through the relevant backup, cloud-storage, or sharing service.

For information handled by Google, use Google's privacy and advertising controls. For a question or request concerning WMS, use the contact mechanism below.

10. Security

WMS processes core media features locally and keeps its private settings and project data in Android's app storage. Exported files are saved in shared storage so you can use them outside WMS.

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted during transmission using TLS. This does not mean that local projects or exported files have additional encryption provided by WMS.

No storage system or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's privacy

WMS is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. If you have a concern about a child's information in connection with WMS, contact the developer using the mechanism in section 15 without posting the child's personal information publicly.

If the intended audience or data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

  • Use Android's advertising settings to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported.
