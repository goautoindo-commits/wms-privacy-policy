WMS · Winterman Music Spectrum

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WMS (Winterman Music Spectrum) accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Android application.

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Last updated: September 29, 2026

Public Google Play Privacy Policy

1. Overview

WMS is a music player and music-visualization application.

WMS can play audio, generate spectrum visualizations, use user-selected images or videos as backgrounds, synchronize text and quotes, save local projects, and export media.

Music files, selected media, visualization processing, project information, and generated exports are primarily processed locally on your device.

WMS does not operate a cloud service for uploading your personal music or project files.

2. Information WMS accesses

Information / Access Purpose Handling
Audio or music files selected by you Playback, visualization, synchronization, and export Processed locally on the device. WMS does not upload selected music to a WMS server.
User-selected images or videos Backgrounds for preview and exported content Processed locally on the device unless you choose to share exported media through another service.
Audio analysis permission Analyze playback audio for spectrum visualization WMS does not intentionally store voice recordings and does not upload analyzed audio to a WMS server.
Local projects, quote timing, settings, and preferences Save and resume projects and restore configuration Stored locally in app storage on your device.
Files generated by WMS MP4 or media export Saved to your device at your request.
Purchase and entitlement information Activate, restore, or verify paid access Processed through Google Play Billing.
Advertising and device data in the Free version Advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention May be processed by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

The Free version of WMS may display advertising using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, SDK configuration, and Google's services, the advertising SDK may collect or process information including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device identifiers, including advertising or app-scoped identifiers where available;
  • app and advertisement interactions such as app launches, taps, impressions, or video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information such as crashes, app performance, SDK performance, hang rate, or energy usage.

Google may process this information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

More information:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google My Ad Center

4. Paid plans and Google Play Billing

WMS may offer paid access through Google Play.

Available purchase options may include:

  • Monthly subscription
  • Annual subscription
  • Lifetime one-time purchase

Payments are processed by Google Play.

WMS does not directly collect or store your complete debit-card, credit-card, or bank-account information.

Google Play may provide WMS with purchase information needed to determine whether paid features should be available.

This may include information such as product identifiers, purchase state, purchase tokens, and entitlement status.

Subscription renewal, cancellation, refunds, payment processing, and account management are handled according to Google Play policies.

5. Data sharing

WMS does not sell your personal music files, selected background media, quote text, project files, or locally generated exports.

Information may be processed by third-party services where required to provide their functionality.

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Google Play Billing for subscriptions, purchases, entitlement verification, and transaction functions.
  • Android / Google Play services where required for operating-system and store functionality.

If you manually share exported media using another application, website, messaging service, cloud service, or social network, that third party's privacy policy applies to the information you choose to share.

6. Data retention and deletion

WMS local project data, preferences, caches, and other app-local information are generally retained on your device until you delete the relevant data, clear app storage, or uninstall WMS.

Exported media remains in the location where you saved it until you delete it.

WMS does not currently provide a WMS cloud account containing your personal music or project files.

Information processed by Google AdMob, Google Play, or other Google services is retained according to Google's applicable policies and legal obligations.

7. Security

WMS minimizes remote handling of user-created media by processing core music, visualization, synchronization, project, and export functions locally on the device.

Third-party services may transmit information using secured network connections according to their own security practices.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

8. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the WMS features you use, the app may request access related to:

  • audio or media files;
  • device storage where required by older Android versions;
  • foreground media playback;
  • audio analysis required for spectrum visualization.

Permissions are used only for the associated application functionality.

You can review or revoke supported permissions through Android settings.

Revoking a permission may prevent the associated feature from working.

9. Children

WMS is not specifically directed to children under 13.

If WMS's Google Play target audience or advertising implementation changes in a way that requires additional child-directed protections, the application configuration and this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Your choices

  • You choose which local media files are opened by WMS.
  • You can revoke Android permissions through system settings.
  • You can delete local WMS data by deleting projects, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the application.
  • You can manage Google Play subscriptions and purchases through Google Play.
  • Where available, you can manage Google's advertising and privacy choices through Google's privacy controls.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when WMS features, third-party SDKs, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be changed whenever the policy is revised.
